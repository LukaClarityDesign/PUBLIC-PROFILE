# About Luka Clarity Design

Luka Clarity Design (L.C.D.) is an independent brand founded by **Bartosz Luka**.

It brings together digital design, UX/UI thinking, visual communication and exploratory product work under one guiding idea: clarity.

The name connects the founder's name, Luka, with the idea of light and the design value of clarity. The brand's promise is to illuminate complex ideas and turn them into understandable, useful experiences.

## Mission

To help make complex ideas easier to understand and use through clear visual communication, considered interaction design and coherent digital experiences.

## Values

- **Clarity:** reduce unnecessary complexity without removing important meaning.
- **Usability:** design around real user needs and understandable interactions.
- **Consistency:** connect visual language, information structure and behavior.
- **Practicality:** favor solutions that serve a purpose over novelty for its own sake.
- **Responsibility:** treat technology, automation and AI as tools that require thoughtful use and appropriate human oversight.

L.C.D. is a parent brand. Its initiatives may differ in subject, maturity and scope; each should be evaluated according to its own status and evidence.
