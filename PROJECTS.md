# Selected Projects and Initiatives

L.C.D. brings together independent projects at different stages of development. This is a selective public overview, not a complete internal project register.

## WORKERCHECK.BE

**Website:** [https://workercheck.be](https://workercheck.be)

A Belgian project focused on helping workers understand their payslips and the components of their remuneration. The web application provides an MVP for screening pay documents and highlighting items that may need attention. It supports Polish, Dutch, English, Ukrainian and Romanian.

The project combines document analysis with structured checks against relevant Belgian employment and payroll rules. Legal conclusions require traceable authoritative data and explicit handling of uncertainty.

**Status:** public website live; application at MVP / release-candidate stage. It is not presented as a certified payroll-compliance engine or as a substitute for professional legal or payroll advice.

## Application Audit Framework (AAF)

An exploratory framework project focused on structured application audits, evidence-based quality assessment and reproducible technical checks.

Have an application, product or idea whose quality and behavior you'd like to examine? **Let's talk.**

**Status:** research, methodology and prototype exploration. This is not presented as a finished product, certified standard or production-approved service.
